Privacy & Data Governance Policy

NischayDrop is engineered on an absolute Zero-Server principle. We do not inspect, intercept, store, or monetize your documents. Tap each clause below to review our technical protocols and statutory protections.

Effective: October 2026 • Jurisdiction: Republic of India (DPDP Act Aligned)

Welcome to NischayDrop, a mission-critical utility developed under the Nischay Ecosystem. This platform provides an instantaneous, direct bridge between citizen mobile devices and commercial print terminals (cyber cafes, stationery hubs, academic institutions, and administrative centers) without the traditional risks of contact disclosure or data leakage.

Historically, printing official paperwork (e.g., Aadhaar identification cards, PAN credentials, land registry certificates, bank statements, or academic degrees) required consumers to register the private mobile phone numbers of unknown shop operators on WhatsApp, or send personal attachments via unencrypted public email terminals. This resulted in systematic identity exposure, permanent hard-disk file residue, and unauthorized contact harvesting.

Core Covenant: NischayDrop completely eliminates this intermediary surface. We do not act as a storage repository. We function exclusively as a mathematical, ephemeral signaling pipeline that connects the sender's web browser directly to the recipient's local browser memory.

Unlike cloud-storage services (Google Drive, WhatsApp Web, Telegram, Dropbox), NischayDrop does not maintain, rent, or operate a central database or file-holding server.

  • Peer-to-Peer (WebRTC) Transport: Data chunks are transmitted directly across encrypted WebRTC data channels negotiated directly between the customer’s phone and the counter display.
  • Transient RAM Execution: When a file arrives at the receiving console, it is reconstructed exclusively within the volatile memory (RAM) of the destination browser using HTML5 Blob objects.
  • Zero Persistence on Physical Disk: The document is never silently written or saved to the operating system's local hard drive or Download folders during transmission.

Privacy begins with identity decoupling. NischayDrop does not demand, collect, or transmit personally identifiable contact tokens:

  • Zero Account Requirement: There are no sign-ups, passwords, social logins, or OTP (One-Time Password) validations required.
  • No Phone Number Exposure: Senders and receivers connect entirely through ephemeral 6-character room badges or cryptographic QR matrix hashes. Neither party sees the other’s SIM or mobile number.
  • Protection of Social Footprints: Senders prevent third-party access to their personal WhatsApp Profile Photos, status updates, or last seen indicators.

To prevent residual extraction after a document is printed, NischayDrop incorporates deterministic cleanup mechanisms:

  • Manual Burn Command: The shopkeeper dashboard features a dedicated "🔥 Destroy (Wipe)" command. Triggering this button invokes URL.revokeObjectURL(), permanently releasing memory pointers and rendering recovery impossible.
  • Tab-Closure Evaporation: Closing the active browser window immediately flushes all loaded documents from active device memory with zero file recovery trails.
  • Silent Print Isolation: Direct print calls route rendering instructions into an isolated DOM sandbox without spawning temporary cache files.

NischayDrop is architected to exceed the stringent requirements established under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023:

  • Zero Data Fiduciary Liability: Since NischayDrop never stores, indexes, or processes personal data on central hardware, it operates purely as an infrastructure conduit rather than a data repository.
  • Purpose Limitation: Transferred files exist solely for the immediate purpose of local physical printing as intended by the data principal (the customer).
  • Right to Erasure by Design: Instant manual memory revocation ensures compliance with the right to data minimization and automatic erasure.

We reject the surveillance capitalism model that dominates today's web utilities:

  • No persistent tracking cookies, fingerprinting pixels, or ad-broker scripts (such as Meta Pixel or Google Analytics advertising trackers) are deployed.
  • We do not log transferred file names, page numbers, internal document content, or device identifiers.
  • Your personal identification cards will never be mined or used to train third-party artificial intelligence models.

While NischayDrop secures the digital transmission channel, cyber cafe owners and printing kiosk operators are subject to the following professional standards:

  • Operators must not capture screen recordings or photos of incoming customer documents using secondary mobile devices.
  • Operators are urged to execute the "🔥 Destroy" button immediately after physical paper prints are collected by the customer.
  • Any physical misprints or paper jams containing sensitive personal info should be shredded responsibly.
